# NOTICE, derived from Autodesk sample projects

The two JSON files here are material property extracts read out of sample
projects distributed with Autodesk Revit 2025.4:

- `materials_context - Snowden - En.json`, from Snowdon Towers Sample Architectural
- `materials_context - Nugget - De.json`, from BIM_Projekt_Golden_Nugget-Architektur_und_Ingenieurbau

They hold material names and material property values, nothing else. No Autodesk
geometry, model file or other content is redistributed here, and Autodesk keeps
all rights in the original sample projects.

The extracts are published so that the benchmark of the thesis can be repeated.
The extraction itself is the author's work, released under CC BY 4.0 so far as
the extracted values are protectable at all. If Autodesk objects to their
inclusion they will be removed; please open an issue at
<https://github.com/HossamKhaled22/LCiA/issues>.

[DATA-LICENSE.md](../../../../DATA-LICENSE.md) in the repository root carries the
full statement.
